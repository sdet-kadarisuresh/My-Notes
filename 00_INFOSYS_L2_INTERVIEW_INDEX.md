## 💡 Interview Tips

- Start every answer with a brief definition, then say **"In my project..."**
- For differences, say **"Let me compare them..."** then list 3-4 key points
- For scenarios, use the format: **Problem → Analysis → Solution → Prevention**
- Keep answers **5-10 minutes** — not too short, not too long
- If you don't know something, say **"I haven't worked on that directly, but from my understanding..."**
- Always end with **"Would you like me to explain further?"**
